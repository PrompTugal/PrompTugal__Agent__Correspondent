# Draft for the journalist: the London Tube drivers' strikes, November 2026 to February 2027

Beat: Europe. From cl-6.correspondent@PrompTugal.dinis, 2026-11-26T15:50Z. For cl-6.journalist, for Dinis's request of 26 Nov 2026. Neutral: no side taken. LinkedIn News (https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/first-of-eight-tube-strikes-hits-london-commuters-8994345/) was the pointer only; it is not cited for any fact.

## Facts, each with its source (all read on 26 Nov 2026)

1. **Who and how many.** Tube drivers in the RMT union are to strike in a series of eight dates announced between November 2026 and February 2027. Source: TfL press release PN-259, 23 Nov 2026, "TfL advises customers to plan ahead of strike action on the London Underground network", read on TfL's press-office host https://tfl-newsroom.prgloo.com/news/tfl-advises-customers-to-plan-ahead-of-strike-action-on-the-london-underground-network (the same release at tfl.gov.uk returned HTTP 403 to our fetch, so the tfl.gov.uk copy was not read).
2. **The eight windows** (each from 12:00 to 11:59 the next day, per TfL's notes to editors; TfL says more customer information will come closer to each date): 26 to 27 Nov 2026; 17 to 18 Dec; 19 to 20 Dec; 4 to 5 Jan 2027; 6 to 7 Jan; 17 to 18 Feb; 23 to 24 Feb; 25 to 26 Feb. Disruption is expected before and after each window. Same TfL source. Time Out (secondary, 26 Nov 2026) lists the same eight dates and notes that dates after the first may be called off: https://www.timeout.com/london/news/tube-and-train-strikes-christmas-winter-2026-2027-full-list-of-dates-and-lines-110626
3. **Lines and services, first strike (TfL).** Normal service until about 10:00 on Thu 26 Nov; disruption from 10:00; most services expected to run but with disruption on all lines from about 12:00. No service on the Circle or Piccadilly lines throughout the day, and none on the Waterloo & City line after midday. On Fri 27 Nov services start from about 07:00, disruption across all lines until about midday, back to normal by about 18:00. The Elizabeth line, DLR, London Overground, buses and trams are expected to run, possibly busier than usual. TfL has not published line detail for strikes 2 to 8; it says it will give more information nearer each date.
4. **The dispute's stated cause (TfL).** "Revised proposals for a voluntary four-day week working pattern for train drivers." TfL says it changed the options so drivers can remain on a five-day week with a guarantee of staying at the same depot.
5. **What TfL says.** Nick Dent, Director of Customer Operations for London Underground: "It is disappointing that the RMT has called this strike action"; "We continue to believe that the majority of the concerns from the RMT have now been resolved and we urge the RMT to put this proposal to their members." TfL also says ASLEF has accepted the four-day week proposal; that is TfL's claim and was not checked elsewhere. Source: TfL PN-259 above.
6. **What the RMT says (secondary, not yet at the primary).** Time Out (26 Nov 2026) quotes general secretary Eddie Dempsey: "London Underground must withdraw these damaging proposals and engage seriously with the union to reach a negotiated settlement", and that "there is nothing voluntary about a scheme that forces drivers to give up existing protections." Time Out also says talks on Tuesday 24 Nov failed. The RMT's own site (https://www.rmt.org.uk/news/) returned HTTP 503 twice, so the union's own announcement has NOT been read: the journalist should re-fetch it before publishing, or attribute the quotes to Time Out.
7. **Separate action.** Time Out says a separate strike on 20 to 21 Nov (Piccadilly and District lines) was by another union (TSSA) and is not part of the eight. Secondary; check before use.
